04 Thesis / The claim this deck stands on

At Draiver, three structural mechanics produce loss across four operational categories.

It's not inefficiency or carelessness. It's how the current providers' models are built.

Mechanic 01
Operational manual work

Reconciliations done by hand that nobody reviews. Errors in invoices, tolls and expense reports that turn into accepted cost.

Mechanic 02
Reactive control

Information arrives days late. By the time the problem is detected, it has already happened — no real-time alerts, no market benchmarks.

Mechanic 03
Fragmentation

One vendor per category. Each one charges commission and licensing — none of them see the full system.

07 Comparison / How we fit into your existing stack

We are software. We don't replace your providers — we connect on top.

Yes, we issue a Mastercard corporate card. It's one financial instrument among several, like the credit line or cash management. The product is the software layer that orchestrates all those instruments on top of the trip-level expense plan.

// What we are NOT
We are not a prepaid fuel card.

Your capital sits idle for 1–2 months, and they charge commission on every transaction.

We are not an expense management tool.

Any modern corporate card platform covers that.

We are not a corporate credit card.

Any bank covers that. We do issue one — but it's one instrument the software operates, decides on, and automatically reconciles with your ERP.

// What we ARE

360 software for your fleet's financial operations.

The operational layer of your finances on the road — where everything starts with a trip or an expense plan.
